Chrome extensions, on macOS 15.4 or later: on a Chrome Web Store page the button now says Add to Search, and uBlock Origin Lite, Bitwarden, Dark Reader, Grammarly and most of the popular ones run on the engine Safari uses. Tabs you haven't looked at for half an hour go to sleep and give their memory back, then come back where you left them. A link clicked in another app while Search is closed now opens its window. Lots of tabs shrink to their icons, then scroll inside the row instead of sliding under the window's buttons. The traffic lights sit where a Mac app's do, a double-click on the title bar fills the screen, and the corners are the standard ones. Downloading a file no longer shows an error page. A new icon, a new disk image, and an app down to 2.9 MB.

Passkeys: sites that offer one can now sign you in with Touch ID or an iCloud passkey — Apple granted Search the browser entitlement for it. A pinned tab closed with ⌘W really lets its page go and comes back whole, instead of a blank that needed a reload. Pinned tabs fill the sidebar's width and can be dragged into any order. Two fingers tapped twice frame what they point at. Right-click on a picture gets a menu whose Copy Image and Download Image actually do it. Bookmarks drag into folders and have a right-click menu. Dark mode, and every panel on one plate. Passwords you bring in from Dia, Chrome, Arc, Brave or Edge read back correctly. The app looks for updates every hour while it is open. Cosmos scrolls again. And a real icon.

The first one.
